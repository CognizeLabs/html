Renewals: Upload last year’s claims. The agent spots cost drivers and recommends design shifts—perhaps a lean level-funded model or a zero-deductible Surest® option—so benefits track real-world utilization and keep the client on our books.

What once required a week of spreadsheets becomes a 60-second, evidence-based conversation.

Commercialization: We can expose the same engine as an API to benefit marketplaces such as Employee Navigator and Ease, where brokers already quote across UHC, Aetna, and Cigna. By powering their recommendations, we create a new SaaS revenue stream while keeping UnitedHealth Group plans front and center.
