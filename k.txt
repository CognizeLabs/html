Who I am / what I lead: AI/ML engineering + solution architecture leader for our Employer & Individual business, partnering closely with business and tech leaders.
My north star: Scale AI-assisted experiences that measurably improve outcomes—speed, quality, and cost-to-serve—without increasing risk.
Where I’m spending time now: Turning high-value workflows into reusable, production-grade AI patterns (not one-off pilots).
Outcome lens: Focus on workflows that move the needle—cycle time reduction, fewer handoffs, call/email deflection, error reduction, better user experience.
Operating model belief: Smaller, empowered cross-functional pods embedded with business drive faster value than heavy ceremony.
How to keep speed + control: “Paved roads” (reference architectures, templates, shared components) so teams don’t reinvent governance/security each time.
Developer productivity: Use Copilot/Codex-type tools to increase throughput, while measuring quality (lead time, PR cycle time, defect escape rate, security findings).
AI security emphasis: Conversational agents create a new attack surface—prompt injection, tool misuse, data leakage—that won’t show up in demos.
Testing stance: Domain-driven testing is essential—each domain owns realistic user intents + adversarial prompts + edge cases in CI/CD.
Red-teaming: We should normalize red-team exercises and continuous evaluation—not just pre-release checks.
Cross-team collaboration: Enterprise support is helpful, but domains need security champions and shared playbooks to close gaps consistently.
What “done” looks like: Least-privilege tool access, injection/data-leak tests, evaluation evidence, audit logs, monitoring/observability, rollback/kill-switch plan.
Question to the CIO: “How do you want teams to partner on AI risk—formal red-team service, or a federated model with domain security champions plus enterprise oversight?”
What I’d ask leadership to enable: A lightweight, shared AI Security & Evaluation Kit (playbooks, reusable tests, definition-of-done) to standardize safety across teams.
